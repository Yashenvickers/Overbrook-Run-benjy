LeftoverIQ on iPhone

A clickable preview of the LeftoverIQ iPhone app. Each screen copies the app's layout and uses the same recipes, ingredients and swaps as the real app's code.

  • Tap the tabs at the bottom to switch screens.
  • Swipe left on a kitchen item to mark it Used or Tossed.
  • Tap a recipe to see what you have, what's missing, and swaps.
  • Check off groceries, then put them away in your kitchen.
This is a preview page, not the app itself. It starts with sample food, and nothing you change is saved. In the real app, photo recognition happens on the iPhone; here you can try it with a sample photo.
