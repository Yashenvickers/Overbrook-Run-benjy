# LeftoverIQ: Start Here

This package contains the LeftoverIQ iPhone app: the full source code, the app icon, the App Store text, a privacy policy template, and the concept website.

## What's inside

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| `LeftoverIQ.xcodeproj` | The Xcode project. Double-click it on a Mac to open the app. |
| `LeftoverIQ/` | The app's source code, icon and settings. |
| `README-DEVELOPER.md` | Step-by-step guide for building, testing and submitting to the App Store. |
| `APP_STORE_LISTING.md` | Ready-to-paste App Store name, subtitle, description, keywords and review notes. |
| `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` | Privacy policy to edit and publish on a website (Apple requires one). |
| `concept-website/index.html` | The LeftoverIQ concept page. Open it in any browser. |

## What the app does today (version 1.0)

All of this works on the phone itself, with no account, server or monthly cost:

- **Kitchen inventory**: fridge, freezer and pantry, with suggested storage spots and expiration dates.
- **Expiration reminders**: notifications before food is expected to expire.
- **Smart grocery list**: warns when you may already have an item at home; checked-off items move straight into the kitchen.
- **Photo-to-recipe**: take or choose a photo; the phone recognizes common ingredients and suggests recipes.
- **What to cook**: 22 built-in recipes ranked by what you own and what's expiring soon.
- **Ingredient substitutions**: swaps for missing ingredients, highlighting the ones already in your kitchen.
- **Personalization**: vegetarian, dairy-free and quick-meal filters; recipes you cook often rank higher.
- **Recipes and sharing**: write your own recipes and share any recipe by message, email or AirDrop.

## What needs more work later

Two features from the original vision need a server and business agreements, so they aren't in version 1.0:

1. **Importing purchases from grocery store apps.** This needs partnership or API access from each store.
2. **A live community feed with chefs and other users.** This needs accounts, a server, and moderation tools (Apple requires reporting and blocking for user-generated content).

The app is designed so these can be added later without starting over.

## What the publisher needs before launch

1. **A Mac with Xcode** (free from the Mac App Store), version 16 or newer.
2. **An Apple Developer Program membership** ($99 per year) at developer.apple.com, in the name of whoever will own the app.
3. **A public web page for the privacy policy and a support contact.** A free site works.
4. **About an afternoon** to follow `README-DEVELOPER.md`, plus Apple's review time (often 1 to 3 days).

> This code was written without access to a Mac, so it has not yet been compiled. A developer opening it in Xcode for the first time may need to fix a few small build errors before it runs. Test it on a real iPhone before submitting.
