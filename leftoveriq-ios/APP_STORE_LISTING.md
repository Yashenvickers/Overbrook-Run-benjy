**Description**:

Stop wasting food. Stop wondering what to cook.

LeftoverIQ is your everyday kitchen assistant. It keeps track of what's in your fridge, freezer and pantry, reminds you what needs to be used, and suggests meals you can make with what you already have. No special refrigerator or extra equipment: just your phone.

KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE
• Track your fridge, freezer and pantry in one place
• LeftoverIQ suggests where each item goes and when it typically expires
• See everything that should be used soon at a glance

USE IT BEFORE YOU LOSE IT
• Get a reminder before food reaches its expected expiration date
• Choose when you're reminded: on the day, or 1 to 3 days before
• See how many items you've used in time

SHOP SMARTER
• Build your grocery list in seconds
• Get a heads-up when you may already have something at home
• Check items off, then put them away in your kitchen with one tap

KNOW WHAT TO COOK
• Snap a photo of your ingredients for instant recipe ideas
• Recipes ranked by what you own and what's expiring first
• Missing something? Get smart substitutions, including ones already in your kitchen
• Add anything you still need to your shopping list with one tap

MADE FOR YOU
• Vegetarian, dairy-free and quick-meal filters
• Recipes you cook often move up your list
• Write your own recipes and share them with friends and family

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your kitchen stays on your phone. No account required, and photos are analyzed on your device.

Know what you have. Know what to cook. Waste less. Save more.
