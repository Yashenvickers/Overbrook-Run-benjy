# LeftoverIQ Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

[COMPANY OR DEVELOPER NAME] ("we") built LeftoverIQ to work privately on your device.

## Information we collect
**None.** LeftoverIQ does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Information stored on your device
Your kitchen inventory, shopping list, recipes, preferences and cooking history are stored only on your device. They are removed when you use **Settings → Delete all my data** or delete the app. If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup), this data may be included in your backups under Apple's terms.

## Camera and photos
If you choose to scan ingredients, the photo is analyzed on your device to recognize ingredients. Photos are not uploaded or saved by LeftoverIQ. You can change camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

## Notifications
Expiration reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Sharing
When you choose to share a recipe or shopping list, the text goes to the app or person you select through the standard iOS share sheet.

## Children
LeftoverIQ is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that collect data (such as accounts or a community feed), we will update this policy and the app's privacy details before that release.
