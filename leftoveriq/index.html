How it fits together

From the store to the plate, in one loop.

Shop

Build a checklist, or import eligible in-store, pickup and delivery purchases from supported grocery apps.

Stock

Items land in your fridge, freezer and pantry inventory, each with an expected expiration date.

Cook

Get recipes built from what you already own, snap a photo for ideas, and swap anything that's missing.

Waste less
